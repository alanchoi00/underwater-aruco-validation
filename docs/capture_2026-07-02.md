## What was NOT recorded, and what it cost

- **`/zed/zed_node/odom` and `/zed/zed_node/pose`** — ZED's visual-inertial odometry. Their
  absence is the defining constraint on this dataset: with no measured range and no VIO,
  **there is no ground truth at all**. Detection range can only be reported against the
  ArUco pose itself (the thing under test), and translation accuracy is not measurable --
  only single-marker-vs-board-reference self-consistency. Both topics were present in the
  day's script but did not make it into the bag.
- **Right lens** — mono only, so no stereo baseline. A stereo range estimate would have been
  a marker-size-independent cross-check.
- **DVL, sonar, MAVROS, tf** — listed in the day's script, absent from the bags, so those
  drivers were not running. `ros2 bag record` waits silently on a topic that never
  publishes, which is why a long topic list is not evidence of coverage.

## Frame-rate anomaly: the two runs are not comparable

The runs used different capture configurations.

- **test1**: dt median 0.402 s (2.49 Hz), std 0.010 s, **zero** gaps above 2x median. A clean,
  deliberate 2.5 Hz.
- **test2**: dt median **0.067 s (14.9 Hz)** with **107 gaps** above 2x median and std 0.116 s.
  The ZED published at ~15 Hz and the recorder kept 8.77 Hz -- roughly **42% of frames
  dropped**, in bursts.

Cause is almost certainly the raw image stream: `960x540 bgra8` is 2.07 MB/frame, so 15 Hz
demands ~31 MB/s sustained. test1 stayed clean only because 2.5 Hz needs ~5 MB/s.

Consequences for analysis: test1's detection-rate statistics rest on a uniform sample;
test2's are thinned unevenly by bursty drops. Do not pool them, and do not read test2's
frame counts as an exposure denominator without accounting for the drops.

## Fixes carried into `recorder/record_bag.sh`

1. Record `/zed/zed_node/odom` and `/zed/zed_node/pose`. Two lines, and they convert this
   from a self-consistency study into a measurable one.
2. Record `/compressed` images, not raw. Removes the 42% drop.
3. Comment out the sensor groups that are not confirmed live, and check with
   `ros2 topic hz` before the dive rather than trusting the list.
